2026 – now · ECE496 capstone

Robot leg power architecture: hip, knee, ankle

A mounted robot leg with three BLDC joints on a 48 V bus. We are comparing a plain battery-powered version with one that adds a supercapacitor bank through a bidirectional converter. The supercap covers short peak loads and absorbs regenerated energy when the joints brake. The two versions are compared on peak battery current, bus sag, energy per movement and recovered energy.

48 Vsupercapacitorbidirectional DC-DCBLDC / FOCBMS
What's in the full block diagram
  • 18650 pack with BMS: cell taps, NTCs, charge/discharge FETs
  • Supercap stack with monitoring and balancing, connected through a bidirectional DC-DC with precharge
  • Per joint: fuse, 3-phase inverter, FOC controller, BLDC motor, gearbox, encoder
  • Power-distribution sense board (bus, battery and supercap V and I) and a brake chopper
  • Hardwired safety interlock (E-stop, guard switch, reset) that works without the MCU
  • RTOS on the main MCU, with telemetry logged to a PC alongside a MuJoCo simulation

48 V series-stacked GPU power delivery

Eight GPU loads stacked in series across a 48 V bus, so each one sees about 6 V and the usual 48→12 V intermediate stage is removed. A switched-capacitor ladder keeps the node voltages balanced when the loads don't match. I worked on the high-current board: stackup, placement, power planes and DC-loss analysis.

FPGA SoM carrier board

A carrier for an FPGA system-on-module that plugs in through three 100-pin board-to-board connectors. A barrel jack feeds a 5 V buck (ADP2384) and a 5-channel PMIC (ADP5052) for the module's I/O and analog rails, with power-good and enable sequencing.

14 MHz Class-E RF power amplifier

A switching RF power amplifier that delivers 1 W at 14 MHz. A 3rd-order Chebyshev band-pass filter keeps THD under 10%. I took it from simulation through PCB layout to bench testing, used PyVISA scripts to automate the measurements, and integrated it into a radio transceiver.

Digital brushed-DC gear-motor controller

A motor-control board I designed and assembled. The STM32F103 drives an L298N H-bridge with PWM and reads the motor's encoder. An LM2596 steps the 12 V input down to 5 V. The OLED shows live voltage, current, power, mAh and run time, and four buttons set the target.

2020 · high school

Hand-built HI radio telescope

A horn antenna for the 21 cm neutral-hydrogen line, based on Ewen and Purcell's 1951 horn. The aperture is 1.5 m × 1.5 m, made of slotted angle steel and 6 mm stainless mesh. It feeds a hand-made waveguide-to-coax transition with an N-type probe that I modelled in HFSS first.
